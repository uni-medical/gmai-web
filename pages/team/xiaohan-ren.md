## About

Xiaohan Ren is a Research Intern at the **GMAI Lab**, currently studying at the
**University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)**. His work focuses on
the intersection of foundation models and scientific discovery, with a particular
interest in autonomous agents for medical reasoning and workflows.

## Research Interests

- **AI for Science**: applying large models to scientific problem solving and discovery pipelines
- **Medical Agents**: designing agentic systems that can plan, use tools, and reason over clinical and biomedical data
